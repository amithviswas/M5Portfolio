M5-AVR-001 Case study

CareerCompass: AI-Powered Career Mentor

Live Code
The problem
Job hunting means rewriting your résumé, guessing what ATS filters want and practising interviews alone.
What I built
An AI career mentor that analyses a résumé, builds an ATS-friendly version, writes cover letters and runs mock interviews with real-time feedback.
How
Next.js and TypeScript on the front, Genkit with Google AI models for the intelligence, Firebase behind it, ShadCN UI and Tailwind for the interface.
What I learned
How to design around AI output — prompts, loading states and making feedback feel useful instead of generic.
